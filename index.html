About the Practice

Research that
moves teams forward.

Baldi Research is an independent research and operations practice founded by Austin Baldi. We work as embedded partners to product, design, and operations teams, coordinating research studies, managing participant records, synthesizing findings into structured reports, and building the documentation infrastructure that keeps research programs consistent across projects and stakeholders.

The work has spanned games, blockchain developer platforms, and accessibility-focused EdTech in West Africa. The common thread is resource-constrained environments where research has to ship fast, decisions have to be defensible, and there is no room for findings that get filed rather than acted on.

Austin holds a B.A. in Psychology from NC State University and has 3+ years of embedded research and coordination experience. Based in Raleigh, NC.

Currently seeking research coordination, operations, and adjacent roles where structured thinking and clear documentation drive better decisions. Open to full-time, contract, and freelance engagements — remote or Raleigh-area.
